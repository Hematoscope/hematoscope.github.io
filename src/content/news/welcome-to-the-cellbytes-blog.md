We're excited to launch the official Cellbytes news — a space where we share updates on our work, insights from the field of digital pathology, and news from our growing network of research and clinical partners.

## What We're Building

Cellbytes is a digital pathology platform designed to bring AI-assisted analysis to haematology laboratories. Our focus is on automating the classification and quantification of blood cells in a way that is accurate, transparent, and trusted by pathologists.

Behind every slide is a patient. That's why we hold ourselves to the highest standards when it comes to regulatory compliance, clinical validation, and collaboration with the medical community.

## What to Expect Here

On this news, you'll find:

- **Product updates** — new features, improvements, and how they came to be
- **Research highlights** — findings from our ongoing clinical collaborations
- **Technology insights** — how we approach problems in computer vision and AI for pathology
- **Company news** — partnerships, milestones, and the people behind Cellbytes

We believe in building in the open, and this news is part of that commitment. If you have questions, feedback, or just want to talk digital pathology, we'd love to hear from you.
